DATA FILE COLUMNS: what every column of a participant's data file shows
=======================================================================

Applies to the files downloaded from Pavlovia (ValMap_<id>_score<pct>pct_SESSION_<date>.csv,
comma separated) and to the local files (sub-<code>_task-gridvalstruct_<stamp>.tsv, tab
separated). Both have the same 28 columns. Written 2026-10-09 for the 3-step task (aliens,
spaceships, planets); the task itself is explained in TASK_OVERVIEW.txt, more detail in
OUTPUT_FILES.md and SCHEDULE_COLUMNS.md.

HOW THE FILE IS ORGANIZED
-------------------------
* One file per session. One row per screen change (a picture going up, a fixation, an answer,
  ...), plus "meta" rows at the top (facts about the session) and "session_end" rows at the
  bottom (summary and score).
* Empty cells are written as n/a.
* Times are in seconds and MEASURED (when the screen actually appeared and disappeared), never
  copied from the design.
* Each main-task block has its own clock: 0.0 is the moment the block was started with the key
  "t". Onsets are therefore comparable within a block only.
* Every row of a main-task trial repeats that trial's design columns (columns 12 to 28), so any
  single row of a trial tells you what the trial was.
* Intro rows (familiarization, practice, gate questions, overviews) are in the same file; filter
  on trial_type to keep the main task only.

Terms used below
  journey / route  one of the three sequences alien -> spaceship -> planet -> treasure. The routes
                   are named A, B, C after their starting alien and keep that name all session.
  100 route        the route currently worth 100 points; the other two are worth 50.
  clip id          the name of a picture, e.g. aliens_2, spaceships_1, planets_3.
  swap             a structure change: the two 50 routes exchange their ends from one step on.
  observed half    the swapped route whose new transition was shown on the change trial.
  unseen half      the other swapped route ("counterfactual"), whose change was never shown.


THE 28 COLUMNS
--------------

1. subj_id
   The participant code: the Prolific ID online, or the code in the link (e.g. COLL01).

2. block
   Main task: the block, 1 to 6 (32 trials each).
   Familiarization and gate questions: the attempt number (1, 2, 3).
   Practice, overviews, meta and session_end rows: 0.

3. trial
   Main task: the trial number in the session, 1 to 192.
   Gate: the question number. Practice: the practice trial number.
   n/a on block-level rows (block_start, block_end, ...) and meta rows.

4. onset
   Seconds on the block clock when this screen appeared (block_start is 0.0). n/a on meta rows.

5. duration
   Measured seconds the screen stayed up. 0 for instant events (a key press, block start/end).
   Self-paced screens: the time until the participant pressed the key.

6. event
   What this row is (see the EVENT list below), e.g. clip1, outcome, rest_readout, probe,
   response.

7. trial_type
   The type of trial, spelled out:
     establish                    the first main-task trial, a full walk
     value_change                 the 100 moved to another route (V)
     structure_change             two 50 routes swapped their ends (S)
     value_control                looks like a value change, but nothing changed (CV)
     structure_control            looks like a structure change, but nothing changed (CS)
     partial_tracking_value       short trial in the value format, no change, no question (TV)
     partial_tracking_structure   short trial in the structure format, no change, no question (TS)
                                  (TV and TS have a rest since 2026-10-09; before, none)
     tracking                     a full walk, no change (T)
     familiarization, practice, gate, overview   intro screens
     session                      meta and session_end rows

8. stimulus
   What was on screen; its form depends on the event (see the EVENT list). On meta and
   session_end rows: key=value.

9. response
   On "response" rows only: the clip id the participant chose.

10. rt
   On "response" rows only: seconds from the question appearing to the key press.

11. correct
   On "response" and "feedback" rows: 1 = correct, 0 = wrong. A missed question has a
   "no_response" row instead (counted as wrong in the score).

--- columns 12 to 28: the design of the trial, repeated on every row of the trial ---

12. kind
   The trial type in short: EST, V, S, CV, CS, TV, TS, T (meanings as in trial_type above).
   A rest follows every short trial (V, S, CV, CS, TV, TS; TV and TS since 2026-10-09) and the T
   right after every change (the follow-up). A question follows the rest on all of them except
   TV and TS.
   What each kind SHOWS:
     EST, T     the whole route (3 pictures) and then its treasure with its points
     V, CV      no picture, only the treasure of the 100 route with "100"
     TV         no picture, only a treasure with its points (100 or 50, half each)
     S, CS, TS  two neighboring pictures of a 50 route, no treasure
   ("gate" appears here on the gate question rows.)

13. probe_slot
   Which question this trial carries:
     change             the question on a change trial (after its rest)
     followup           the question on the trial right after a change (after its own rest)
     control            the question on a control trial (CV, CS)
     practice_question  the three swap questions in the practice (not scored)
   n/a when the trial has no question.

14. winner_column
   Which route (A, B or C) is worth 100 after this trial. The other two are worth 50.
   On a value change, the previous trial's winner_column is the route that lost the 100.

15. untouched_column
   On change trials only: the route the change did not involve (the within-trial control).
   Value change: the 50 route that stayed 50. Structure change: the 100 route.

16. swap_step
   On structure changes only: where the two routes were cut and swapped. 1->2 means the
   spaceships and planets were exchanged (each alien now boards the other route's spaceship);
   2->3 means only the planets were exchanged.

17. counterfactual_route
   On structure changes only: the route of the UNSEEN half of the swap (its new transition was
   never shown). The observed half is the other swapped route, the one whose pictures appear in
   shown_videos.

18. shown_videos
   The pictures shown on this trial, in order (separated by |). Empty on V, CV and TV (they show
   only the treasure).

19. hidden_videos
   The pictures of the walked route that were NOT shown on this trial.

20. route_A_videos
21. route_B_videos
22. route_C_videos
   The three journeys as they stand AFTER this trial, pictures in step order, e.g.
   aliens_3|spaceships_2|planets_1. Each route's treasure is the one belonging to its planet
   (see the reward_images meta row, which gives the treasure of each starting route's planet).

23. reactivation_targets
   On change trials only: the pictures predicted to be replayed in the rest after the change
   (the parts of the changed routes that were not shown). Value change: the new 100 route and the
   route that lost the 100. Structure change: the unshown parts of both swapped routes.

24. probe_type
   What the question tests:
     value_unobserved        after a value change: which picture do you prefer? The answer is a
                             picture of the new 100 route that was not shown
     value_established       value control: a picture of the current 100 route, any step
     structure_unobserved    after a structure change: which picture belongs to the same journey
                             as the cue? The answer was never shown and was created by the swap
     structure_established   structure control: same question about a pairing already seen
   (value_observed and structure_observed are older names for the controls, used in the first
   pilot rounds and on some practice rows.)

25. probe_subtype
   On structure change and follow-up questions: observed_leg (about the observed half) or
   counterfactual_leg (about the unseen half).

26. probe_options
   The three answer options, in screen order left to right (keys J, K, L), separated by |.
   All three are at the same step (same category), one from each route.

27. probe_correct_video
   The correct option.

28. probe_cue_video
   The picture shown above a structure question ("which belongs to the same journey as this?").
   n/a on value questions, which show only the options.


EVENT VALUES (column 6) AND WHAT stimulus HOLDS
-----------------------------------------------
meta              a session fact, stimulus = key=value (list below)
block_start       the "t" key that starts a block; onset 0.0; stimulus = date and time
block_lead_in     the 3 s fixation before the block's first trial
fixation_start    the big cross that opens every trial; stimulus = scheduled=<seconds>
                  (the planned duration; duration column = what it really took)
clip1, clip2, clip3   a picture on screen; stimulus = its clip id
fixation_gap      the small cross between screens; stimulus = scheduled=<seconds>
outcome           the treasure screen; stimulus = treasure+points, e.g. gold+100
rest_readout      the rest (replay window): fixation only, 5 s online
probe             the question screen; stimulus = the three options; its duration is the
                  time it stayed up (answer time plus a short highlight, or the full 8 s)
response          the key press; response, rt and correct are filled in
no_response       the 8 s window ran out without an answer
feedback          the "Correct!" / "Wrong." screen; correct = 1 or 0
practice_note     an explanation screen in the practice; stimulus = the text key
structure_overview  the screen with the three starting journeys before the main task;
                  stimulus = the three routes A;B;C
break_overview    the screen with the current journeys in each break between blocks
block_end         end of the block
page_hidden       online only: the participant switched tab or window; duration = how long
fullscreen_exit   online only: the participant left fullscreen
reminder          online only: the reminder shown after either of the two above
session_end       a summary value at the end, stimulus = key=value (list below)


META ROWS (top of the file, event = meta)
-----------------------------------------
subj_id, schedule (which of the 100 schedules), steps (3), modality, implementation
(jspsych or psychopy), language (en/de), dry_run (true = a test run, exclude), timing_profile,
layout (which picture sits in which grid cell, e.g. A1=aliens_3|A2=spaceships_1|...),
reward_images (the treasure of each starting route, A=money|B=gold|C=diamonds), timings (all
planned durations), main_task_feedback, skipped_phases (intro parts skipped by a test link;
empty for real participants), data_sink, schedule_assignment (url = from the link, hash_of_id =
from the participant ID), prolific_pid, prolific_study_id, prolific_session_id (Prolific only),
log_schema, started.


SESSION_END ROWS (bottom of the file, event = session_end)
---------------------------------------------------------
gate_passed (true/false), gate_attempts (1 to 3), gate_scores (correct out of 5 per attempt,
e.g. 4/5|5/5), n_page_hidden and page_hidden_s (how often and how long they left the page),
n_fullscreen_exit, n_correct and n_questions (main-task questions answered correctly, of 60),
score_pct (the score shown to the participant; a missed question counts as wrong), status
(complete when they reached the end), finished (date and time).


QUICK RECIPES
-------------
* One row per main-task question: keep event = response or no_response with a main-task
  trial_type (not practice or gate). correct, rt, probe_type, probe_slot and probe_subtype are on
  that row.
* What a trial showed: rows with event clip1/clip2/clip3 and outcome for that trial number.
* The state of the world at any trial: winner_column and route_A/B/C_videos on that trial's rows.
* Old answer after a change (a "stale" error): look up the cue's route on the trial before the
  change; the option that belonged to it then is the stale answer.
* Exclude test runs: meta dry_run = true, or status other than complete.
